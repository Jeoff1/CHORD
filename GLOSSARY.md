# CHORD Glossary

- **Tradition** — A Christian tradition preset (Roman Catholic, Alliance, Iglesia Ni Cristo) that configures modules, terminology, roles, service templates, songbooks, themes, and calendar. Avoid: denomination, religion.
- **Church Profile** — The single record describing this installation's church, including its active Tradition.
- **Service Template** — A Tradition's structure for a service (e.g. Mass, Worship Service, Pagsamba) with ordered Song Slots.
- **Song Slot** — A named position in a Service Template that a song fills (e.g. Entrance, Offertory).
- **Songbook** — A hymnal or song library a Song belongs to; Traditions allow specific Songbooks.
- **Theme** — A controlled-vocabulary topic shared by worship plans and songs; common or tradition-specific. Avoid: tag.
- **Service Theme** — The required Theme of a worship plan; the primary Phase 8 recommendation input.
- **Season** — A Church Calendar period (e.g. Lent, Advent) with a date range. Avoid: liturgical period.
- **Occasion** — A Church Calendar event that is not a season (e.g. INC anniversary).
- **Church Record** — A sacramental or church record (baptism, wedding, dedication…) whose types depend on the Tradition.
- **Worship Plan** — An existing model: a planned service with a setlist.
- **Slot Context** — The Service Theme, Season, allowed Songbooks and target Song Slot for one Song Slot of a Worship Plan; the input bundle reserved for Phase 8. Avoid: recommendation, query features.
